About Me

Professional Summary

I am a Digital Copywriter and UX Content Strategist with 4+ years of experience creating user-focused content for digital products, SaaS platforms, marketing funnels, and editorial platforms. With a background in Psychology, I combine user behavior insights with content strategy to create clear, engaging experiences that help users understand products, complete actions, and build trust with brands.

My expertise includes UX writing, product messaging, onboarding experiences, conversion-focused copy, SEO strategy, CRO optimization, email marketing, and research-driven content creation. I specialize in transforming complex ideas into simple, human-centered communication that improves user experience, increases engagement, and supports business growth.

Core tools & technologies: Figma, Notion, Google Analytics 4, Ahrefs, SEMrush, WordPress, Elementor, Prompt Engineering, AI Workflows.

Outside of user flows and conversion rates, I’m an endless student of just about everything. Depending on the day, you’ll find me deep in a sketchpad, writing, or exploring new artistic outlets to keep the creative side of my brain sharp. But here’s the kicker: I’m just as obsessed with clean spreadsheets, raw statistics, and mathematical logic as I am with design. That blend of artistic expression and quantitative rigor isn't just a fun contradiction—it's my secret weapon for building content that feels intuitive, looks great, and hits the metrics every single time.
